How it works

My Team adds a player to your roster. Taken removes that player from search without adding them to you. Bye warnings are advice, not a command to pass on a stud.

IDP: DL accepts DL/DE/DT, DB accepts DB/CB/S, and IDP Flex accepts any supported individual defender. Dual-eligible players can satisfy any matching slot.

Colors: green is healthy, yellow is getting tight, red means you reached/exceeded your preferred bye cap or created a lineup-coverage problem.

Player data: V1.0 refreshes active NFL players from Sleeper at most once per day and caches a compact copy in your browser. Team defenses are generated separately. Sleeper's player API is read-only and supports active-player filtering.
